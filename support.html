常见问题FAQ

免费额度是怎么算的？/ How does the free quota work?

免费可完整生成 3 副图纸。导入照片、生成、编辑全程免费，第一次进入跟拼模式时才计数；同一副图纸重复打开不会重复扣。订阅期内生成不限量。

Three full patterns are free. Import, generation, and editing are always free; counting starts when you first enter Follow Mode, and the same pattern is never counted twice. Subscriptions include unlimited generation.

如何取消订阅或恢复购买？/ How do I cancel or restore a subscription?

取消：系统「设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅」中选择豆夹取消，当前周期内继续有效。恢复：App 内「设置 > 订阅管理 > 恢复购买」，换机或重装后可找回权益。

Cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions (remains active until the period ends). Restore in the app: Settings > Subscription > Restore Purchases.

我的照片会被上传吗？/ Are my photos uploaded?

不会。照片全程只在你设备上处理，App 没有任何网络请求，断网也能使用全部功能。详见隐私政策。

No. Photos are processed entirely on your device; the app makes no network requests and works fully offline. See the Privacy Policy.

导出的图纸为什么有品牌条？/ Why do exports include a brand bar?

导出图边缘附有豆夹品牌标识与小程序码，方便你分享作品时注明出处；不影响图纸本身的跟拼。你可以自行裁剪。

Exported charts carry a small brand bar in the margin. It does not affect the pattern itself, and you may crop it yourself.

支持哪些拼豆色卡体系？/ Which bead palettes are supported?

内置 MARD（291 色，默认）、Perler、Hama、Artkal 四大体系，生成前可在设置中切换，量化直接在目标色卡上完成，色号即买即拼。首次使用按地区自动预置。

MARD (291 colors, default), Perler, Hama, and Artkal. Switch in Settings before generating; quantization runs directly on the target palette. Defaults are preset by region.
