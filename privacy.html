豆夹 Beadit 隐私政策

Beadit Privacy Policy

生效日期 Effective: 2026-10-24

简而言之：豆夹不收集你的任何数据。你的照片全程只在你自己的设备上处理，永不上传。
In short: Beadit collects no data. Your photos are processed entirely on your device and never uploaded.

1. 我们不收集数据We Collect No Data

豆夹 App 内没有任何网络请求、分析统计 SDK、广告组件或追踪代码。我们不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息，也无法识别单个用户。

Beadit contains no network requests, analytics SDKs, advertising components, or tracking code. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and we cannot identify individual users.

2. 照片全端侧处理On-Device Photo Processing

你导入的照片在生成图纸的全过程中（风格化、量化、编辑、清单、导出）只在设备内存与本地存储中处理，App 不会将照片或任何中间数据发送到任何服务器。断网状态下全部功能可用。

Photos you import are processed entirely in your device's memory and local storage throughout the entire pipeline (stylization, quantization, editing, material list, export). Nothing is sent to any server. All features work offline.

3. 本地存储Local Storage

你的偏好设置、编辑器草稿与图纸仅保存在设备本地：卸载 App 即随之清除。唯一例外：免费额度的使用记录保存在系统钥匙串中，卸载重装后不会重置（防止重复领取免费额度），该记录不含任何个人身份信息。

Your preferences, drafts, and patterns are stored locally on your device and removed when you uninstall the app. The only exception: free-quota usage is recorded in the system Keychain so it survives reinstalls (preventing duplicate free quota). That record contains no personal identity information.

4. 订阅与支付Subscriptions & Payments

订阅的购买、续订与退款全部由 Apple 通过你的 Apple 账户处理，豆夹不接触、也收不到你的任何支付信息。订阅状态由 Apple 管理，你可在系统「设置 > Apple 账户 > 订阅」中查看或取消。

Purchases, renewals, and refunds are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple account. Beadit never receives your payment information. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

5. 儿童隐私Children's Privacy

豆夹的年龄分级为 4+。App 不收集任何年龄段的个人数据，包括儿童。家长与孩子可以放心共同使用。

Beadit is rated 4+. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Safe for families to use together.

6. 政策变更Changes to This Policy

如本政策发生实质变更，我们将更新本页面的生效日期。继续使用 App 即表示接受修订后的政策。

If this policy materially changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
